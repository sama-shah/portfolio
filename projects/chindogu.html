Creative Sprint

I built a leash for your skateboard

The Brief

Chindogu is the Japanese art of the "unuseless" — inventions that solve a real problem in a way so inconvenient they can never practically be used. Design and build one.

The Work

A leash for your skateboard. It solves the very real problem of a runaway board — and makes every other aspect of skating completely impossible. Fully prototyped and presented as a legitimate product concept.
