A tea house retirement home

The Brief

Design a retirement home concept built around the ritual and community of tea. How might a space centered on slowness, warmth, and connection reshape how we think about aging?

The Work

An architectural and experiential concept exploring how ritual can structure community life for older adults. The tea house becomes both a space and a daily rhythm — anchoring social connection without forcing it.
