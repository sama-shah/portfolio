Foodie: A fridge that tracks your food expiration dates

The Brief

Design a smart refrigerator that actively tracks the freshness and expiration of its contents — reducing food waste and making meal planning easier without requiring behavior change from the user.

The Work

A product design sprint exploring how ambient intelligence can take the cognitive burden out of food management. The challenge was designing a system that felt invisible during daily use but surfaced exactly the right information at exactly the right time.
