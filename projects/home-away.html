Designing a home away from home

The Brief

What makes a space feel like home when you're far from it? Design an environment — physical or experiential — that replicates the psychological comfort and familiarity of home for someone living away.

The Work

An interior design and experience concept focused on the emotional architecture of belonging. Explored personalization, sensory familiarity, and the balance between novelty and comfort.
