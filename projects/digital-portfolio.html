Design + Dev

A portfolio as a living document, not a static showcase

The Challenge

Most portfolios tell you what someone did. They're galleries — polished, curated, and frozen in time. They don't show the thinking, the pivots, or the taste that produced the work.

I wanted something that felt less like a resumé and more like a journal. A place that grows with me and reflects how I actually approach creative problems.

The Solution

A hand-coded portfolio built without a CMS or template — just HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. The design choices are intentional: large typography, a dark palette with warm accents, editorial layouts that treat content as composition.

An edit mode lets me arrange project elements freely on the canvas, treating the portfolio itself as an ongoing design exercise. The work here is the portfolio.

My Role

Solo designer and developer. From concept to code — visual design, interaction design, and front-end implementation.
