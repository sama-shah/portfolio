Femtech

Making women's health data feel personal, not clinical

The Challenge

Wearable hardware is a solved problem. The real challenge is making women trust what the data is telling them — and actually change behavior because of it.

Most femtech products collect data but fail to translate it into meaningful, contextual insight. They speak in charts and numbers when they should be speaking in lived experience.

The Solution

A wearable designed from the ground up around the user's mental model of her own body — not the medical establishment's. Data surfaces as guidance, not metrics. The product meets women where they are, whether they're athletes, navigating perimenopause, or trying to understand their cycle for the first time.

The design process was heavily research-driven: dozens of interviews, concept tests, and prototype feedback sessions shaped every interaction decision before a line of hardware was spec'd.

My Role

Founder & lead product designer. Responsible for user research strategy, product vision, interaction design, and translating clinical data requirements into consumer-facing experiences.
