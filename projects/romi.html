Industry

The first smart jewelry designed for women going through menopause

The Challenge

Menopause affects over 1.3 billion women worldwide, yet the wearable category remains entirely undesigned for this phase of life. Existing health devices are clinical, generic, and built with a one-size-fits-all model that erases the nuance of menopause.

The challenge wasn't just building a wearable — it was making something women actually want to wear. Something that fits their life, not a hospital waiting room.
