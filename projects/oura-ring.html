Visualized Oura Ring for in-between doctor-patient communication

The Challenge

Most health decisions don't happen in a doctor's office — they happen in the days and weeks between visits. Patients collect rich biometric data via wearables like the Oura Ring, but have no structured way to share or contextualize it with their care team.

The gap between wearable data and clinical care needed a designed bridge.

My Role

Strategist, designer, and developer at the Ellison Institute of Technology. Led visualization strategy, interaction design, and front-end development of the communication layer.
