AI + Product

Embedding AI into how a $10B construction firm thinks about product

The Challenge

A 90-year-old construction company doesn't naturally reach for AI. The challenge wasn't technical — it was cultural. Getting stakeholders across business units to see AI as a tool they shape, not a threat they manage.

Legacy processes, siloed data, and skepticism about automation created real friction. Any approach that started with technology before people was guaranteed to fail.

The Solution

A product innovation program built on empathy first, technology second. Starting with deep listening sessions across departments, I mapped where human judgment was bottlenecked by manual process — then designed AI interventions that augmented rather than replaced that judgment.

The program established a repeatable framework for identifying, validating, and deploying AI-powered product improvements — giving the organization a muscle it didn't have before.

My Role

AI & Product Innovation lead. Responsible for program design, stakeholder alignment, use-case prioritization, and bridging the gap between technical capability and business need.
