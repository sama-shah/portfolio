Algorave digital sequencer

What is Algoraving?

Algorave is a form of live music performance where sound is generated entirely through code — written and modified in real time on stage, projected for the audience to watch. It sits at the intersection of programming, electronic music, and performance art, where the algorithm itself becomes the instrument.

Instead of turning knobs or playing keys, the performer types functions, patterns, and transformations live — mutating loops, layering rhythms, and sculpting sound through logic. The music only exists while the code runs.

My Interface

I built a browser-based visual sequencer that makes algorave-style composition tangible and fast. Rather than writing raw code every session, the interface lets me map out beats, melodies, and modulations visually — then translates that into a live synthesis engine underneath. The goal was to lower the barrier between idea and sound so I could make my own EDM and hyperpop samples without losing the generative, algorithmic nature of the craft.
