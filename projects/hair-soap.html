Creative Sprint · Sustainability

If hair could be recycled into soap bottles

The Brief

Explore a circular material economy concept: what if human hair — one of the most abundant forms of biological waste — could be processed and repurposed as a material for everyday consumer packaging?

The Work

A speculative design sprint that pushed into material science, brand design, and consumer psychology simultaneously. The provocation: sustainability doesn't have to come from obvious materials. Sometimes the most radical solutions are already in the room.
